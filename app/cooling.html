10 cooling centers listed by Kern County Aging & Adult Services for the 2026 season. One is inside Bakersfield; the rest serve the wider county.

County list checked September 8, 2026. Centers open when the National Weather Service forecast reaches 105°F in the San Joaquin and Kern River valleys, 108°F in desert areas or 93°F in Frazier Park, from 1:00 to 8:00 p.m. unless stated otherwise.
