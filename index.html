What is measured, and what is estimated. Surface temperature (Landsat 8/9 thermal) is measured per area. Tree cover in Contra Costa, Bakersfield and San Ramon comes from the USFS / CAL FIRE 2022 California Urban Tree Canopy, a 0.6 m aerial classification of ground actually under tree crowns; where that assessment is missing, a satellite greenness index stands in and the app says so. Population and age are US Census ACS 2020–2024 five-year estimates allocated into areas; air-conditioning access is the Census LACE 2023 tract estimate, weighted zero in San Ramon where it is near-universal. In Contra Costa County surface heat is weighted zero: the county spans marine to Delta, so a hotter area there is mostly a different climate, not a shade problem. Walking time in the three current builds is routed on the OpenStreetMap pedestrian network to mapped libraries, community centers, pools and senior centers, whose hours and cooling status are not verified; the official county cooling directories are listed separately. Tree counts and costs are planning assumptions, not quotes. Every current build carries a data & methods guide with sources and limits.

An exploratory planning screen, not a field survey. Source, pipeline and write-up: github.com/advikar/coolequity · questions and corrections: open an issue.
