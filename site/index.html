What is estimated, and what is measured. Surface temperature (Landsat 8/9 thermal) and vegetation cover (Sentinel-2 NDVI) are measured per block. Population comes from the US Census ACS. Air-conditioning access is modelled from income, not measured, and in San Ramon it is weighted zero because the city's income range is too narrow for that model to say anything real. Walking time to a cooling site is a circuity-adjusted straight line, not a routed street path. Vegetation cover is an NDVI proxy — it cannot tell a mature tree from an irrigated lawn. Tree costs are planning assumptions, not quotes.
